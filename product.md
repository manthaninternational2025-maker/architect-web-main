# Product Specification: Impulse Research Work Portal

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**Impulse Research Work** is a high-end academic and research consultancy portal engineered to support PhD scholars, postgraduate researchers, and academic faculty through every stage of their research lifecycle—from initial proposal and synopsis formulation to thesis editing, statistical data analysis, similarity review, and journal submission.

Unlike generic commercial agencies or AI landing pages, Impulse Research Work is modeled after prestigious international academic journals and research institutions. The platform projects credibility, rigorous academic standards, strict confidentiality, and intellectual integrity.

## 2. Core Value Propositions & Guardrails
- **Academic Support, Not Contract Cheating**: Strict adherence to academic ethics. We provide methodological guidance, structural critique, statistical analysis support, editorial review, and submission preparation.
- **Zero Exaggerated Claims**: 
  - NO guaranteed publication promises.
  - NO guaranteed thesis approvals.
  - NO claims of "0% plagiarism" or "AI detection bypass".
  - Language is purely editorial, consultative, and methodological.
- **Personalized & Timely Support**: Direct scholarly consultation via direct portal requirement submission and WhatsApp channel (+91 8527231973).

## 3. Target Audience Personas
1. **Doctoral Candidates (PhD Scholars)**: Seeking thesis structure review, statistical data analysis (SPSS, R, Python, AMOS, PLS-SEM), and defense/Viva preparation.
2. **Postgraduate & Master's Researchers**: Requiring synopsis drafting support, APA/IEEE referencing standard alignment, and literature review organization.
3. **Faculty & Independent Academics**: Seeking academic editing, similarity diagnostics (Turnitin / Drillbit guidance), and journal formatting/submission support.
